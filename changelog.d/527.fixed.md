Compute inequality Gini coefficients and top-10%, top-1% and bottom-50% income shares with microdf's estimators, which the existing `microdf_python>=1.3.0,<1.4` pin already provides. Gini values change when records have unequal weights, because the previous formula was wrong there: its Lorenz-curve trapezoid subtracted half of each record's population share where half of its income share belongs. Income shares change because the record straddling each cutoff is now split in proportion to its weight instead of falling wholly on one side, so constant incomes give a top-10% share of exactly 10%.
