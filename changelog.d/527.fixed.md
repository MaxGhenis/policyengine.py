Compute inequality Gini coefficients and top-10%, top-1% and bottom-50% income shares with microdf's estimators, and require `microdf_python>=1.5` (was `>=1.3.0,<1.4`). Gini values change because the previous formula was wrong whenever records had unequal weights: its Lorenz-curve trapezoid subtracted half of each record's population share where half of its income share belongs. Income shares change because the record straddling each cutoff is now split in proportion to its weight instead of falling wholly on one side, so constant incomes give a top-10% share of exactly 10%.
